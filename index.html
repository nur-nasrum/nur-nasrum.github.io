Intro

I am an R&D Engineer at Samsung Research Bangladesh, where I work on wearable sensing pipelines and production systems for Galaxy devices. Previously, I was an undergraduate student and research assistant at IUTOIC. My research interest revolves around On-Device AI, Acoustic and Physical Behavior Sensing, Trustworthy ML — developing practical, human-centered technology for health and assistive use cases.
